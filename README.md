# 📖 About The Research

The paper explores how **Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT)** is used as an evidence-based treatment for anxiety disorders and discusses its practical applications in psychology and mental health care.

The research mainly focuses on:

- Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)
- Social Anxiety Disorder (SAD)
- Panic Disorder
- Cognitive Restructuring
- Behavioral Activation
- Exposure Therapy

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the effectiveness of CBT in treating anxiety disorders
- Review existing psychological research and clinical findings
- Understand mechanisms behind CBT techniques
- Explore modern developments and future research directions

# 📄 Abstract Overview

Anxiety disorders are among the most common psychiatric conditions affecting millions worldwide. This paper reviews current research on CBT and explains how therapeutic techniques such as cognitive restructuring and exposure therapy help individuals manage anxiety effectively.

The paper also discusses future possibilities and emerging trends in psychological treatment and research.
